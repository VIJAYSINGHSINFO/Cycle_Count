Stowra privacy policy

Operated by {{COMPANY}} · Last updated {{DATE}}

Stowra is a warehouse operations app used by staff of {{COMPANY}} and its authorised partners for stock counting, order quality checks, and vehicle gate and dock control. This policy explains what the app collects and why. It applies to the Stowra Android app and the Stowra web app.

Who can use the app

Stowra is a business tool. Accounts are created for, and approved by, the company's administrators. It is not intended for children.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, work email address and role, used to sign you in and to record who did each action.
  • Work records: counts, order checks, scans, notes, and the time and device label of each action. These form the audit trail the company relies on.
  • Photos: photos you take of damaged stock or reported items. They are used only as evidence for those records.
  • Vehicle and driver details (gate staff only): vehicle plate, transporter, driver name and mobile number, and the numbers and expiry dates of the driver's Emirates ID, driving licence and vehicle registration (Mulkiya). These are used to control site access and keep a record of each visit.

The app uses the camera only when you scan a barcode or take a photo. It does not collect your location, contacts or other files, and it does not show advertising.

How it is used and shared

The data is used only to run warehouse operations for {{COMPANY}}. It is not sold and not used for advertising. It is stored with the company's database provider (Supabase), encrypted in transit over HTTPS, and visible only to signed-in users of the company according to their role. A driver who receives a gate pass link sees only the pass code, plate, site and time.

How long it is kept

Records are kept for as long as the company needs them for operations, audits and legal obligations, and then deleted according to the company's retention rules.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, or for your account and personal data to be corrected or deleted, by contacting {{EMAIL}}. Some records may need to be kept where the law or an audit requires it.
